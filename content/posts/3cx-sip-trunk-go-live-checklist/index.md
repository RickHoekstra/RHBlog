Moving phone numbers is the one change where every user notices a mistake straight away. A short checklist takes most of the stress out of it.

## Before the porting date

1. Confirm the full list of numbers with the customer, including fax and alarm lines.
2. Set up the trunk in advance and test outbound calls with a temporary number.
3. Build the inbound rules for every number, even though nothing arrives on them yet.
4. Agree on who calls whom if something goes wrong.

## On the day

1. Call each main number from a mobile phone and from a landline.
2. Check the caller ID that is shown on outbound calls.
3. Test the paths outside office hours: voicemail, holiday schedule, queues.

## Afterwards

Keep an eye on the event log for the first few days and ask the customer about missed calls. Problems with a single number often show up only when that number is first used.
